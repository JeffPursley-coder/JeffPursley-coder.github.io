Portfolio / Operations / Safe Haven

Real operating leadership · Safe Haven Security Services

Hand me a struggling team. I'll build the conditions for a turnaround.

At Safe Haven, I led the turnaround of a struggling 15-person team through coaching, onboarding, clear KPIs and better agent-to-technician handoffs. Quarterly team revenue improved approximately 25%. My later scope included three teams, three reporting managers and approximately 48 employees.

Operating challenge

The operating rhythm needed more work than the team needed speeches.

Distributed teams needed clearer expectations for readiness, performance coaching and follow-through on customer commitments. The work was less about individual selling techniques and more about improving the management system around the team.

Employee readiness

Inconsistent training time slowed the transition from new hire to productive field work.

Performance visibility

Teams needed shared KPIs and coaching routines so issues could be identified early and addressed consistently.

Customer delivery

Agent-to-technician communication needed repeatable follow-through to support installation and customer commitments.

What I changed

I don't just run teams. I make the people running them better.

Redesigned onboarding

Structured training and employee readiness, reducing the usual two-to-three-week preparation period to approximately one week.

Established KPI routines

Applied measurable performance expectations, regular reviews and coaching methods to identify the constraint behind performance.

Developed managers

Established management review cadences, accountability and reporting across the broader team structure.

Strengthened handoffs

Standardized communication between agents and technicians so customer obligations had clearer follow-through.

Reported outcomes

The result was bigger than one strong quarter.

Team turnaround

~25% higher quarterly team revenue

The underperforming team improved quarterly revenue after the turnaround initiatives.

Onboarding

2–3 weeks down to ~1 week

New-hire readiness improved through a more structured onboarding and training approach.

Management systems

Broader adoption

Training, KPI reviews, coaching and workflow practices were adopted more broadly with director support.

Results are first-person reported historical operating accomplishments. Private employer dashboards, personnel data and independently audited financial source records are not publicly reproduced here. The 15-person turnaround and broader 48-person regional scope describe different responsibilities.

Transferable value

When the system improves, the team can keep improving.

Understand a performance constraint. Develop the people closest to it. Build practical routines. Coordinate handoffs. Review actual outcomes. This is the same discipline I bring to service operations, project delivery and business systems implementation.
