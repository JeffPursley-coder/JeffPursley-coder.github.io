My delivery approach

Understand the business. Control the execution. Make the handoff work.

Discovery & definition

Map what is happening, interview the people doing the work when available, define requirements and decision rights, and separate the actual constraint from a surface symptom.

Manage the backlog or delivery plan, dependencies, owners, risks, change requests, stakeholder updates, testing decisions and implementation controls.

Adoption & value

The difference in my project approach

I do not see project management as producing a plan and leaving when a tool goes live. My focus is linking delivery to the real operating problem—clear requirements, credible decisions, accountable owners and a handoff the business can sustain.

The implementation documents in my Juniper Vale case are a simulated demonstration, not evidence of a past customer deployment. The Service Manager, customer operations and nonprofit examples describe work with actual teams and workflows.
