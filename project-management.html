Actual systems implementation

A service manager needed fewer inbox decisions, not more technology.

I translated a field-service manager’s daily responsibilities into business rules and configured Google Workspace, Gmail, Gemini and Workspace Studio workflows to surface urgent issues, assign current ownership, and carry unfinished obligations into scheduled management briefings.

Business constraint

Critical work disappeared in inbox activity

Vendor and national-account issues competed with routine messages while the manager was in the field. Replying to an email did not necessarily close the obligation.

Implementation decision

Separate urgency from responsibility

Defined one current work state, an independent urgent flag, owner transitions and review checkpoints. Adjusted rules around actual Workspace Studio constraints.

Measured operational result

50% less manual email triage

The configuration reduced manual sorting time. A separate 30-case synthetic acceptance suite tests the public rules; those tests are not represented as client production results.
