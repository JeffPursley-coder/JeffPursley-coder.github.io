Business-building experience

Dragonfly: building the business while running it.

Co-built and operated the business side of a service company that grew from 4 to 21 employees, with approximately $2.5 million in revenue over two years and about 25% fewer repeat service calls. My responsibilities included hiring, workforce planning, pricing, payroll budgets, scheduling, purchasing and decisions affecting service profitability.

People & capacity

Hiring, onboarding, scheduling, coaching, performance discussions, payroll coordination and workforce planning as the organization expanded.

Service execution

Dispatch, route planning and rerouting, customer escalations, estimates, quality follow-through, technician readiness and coordinating the work when plans changed.

Business infrastructure

Systems and recordkeeping, operating procedures, vendors, purchasing, inventory, equipment and decisions balancing service quality, workload and expense.

I directly managed day-to-day hiring, compensation, scheduling, pricing, equipment and software decisions, while making larger financial, acquisition, payroll-budget and profitability decisions jointly with the other operator. This distinction matters: I was responsible for the execution and contributed to the financial trade-offs behind it.

Regional leadership & team turnaround

Safe Haven: turn around the team, then strengthen the managers.

I turned around an underperforming 15-person team across distributed territories, improving quarterly revenue by approximately 25%. Later I held broader responsibility for three teams, three reporting managers and approximately 48 employees.

I reworked onboarding and coaching, established repeatable KPI and manager routines, and strengthened agent-to-technician handoffs. New-hire readiness improved from two to three weeks to approximately one week.

Other previously reported results included customer stick rate improving from roughly 82% to 89% and chargebacks/reversals declining from roughly 15% to 10%. These are historical reported results, not independently audited public employer data.

Tools included SecurityTrax, Alarm.com and Excel reporting to support performance, installation and customer follow-through decisions.
