Business-building experience

Dragonfly Mosquito & Pest Specialists

Co-built and helped operate a service business that grew to approximately 21 employees. I was involved in far more than supervising technicians: I managed the business processes that made the service operation possible.

People & capacity

Hiring, onboarding, scheduling, coaching, performance discussions, payroll coordination and workforce planning as the organization expanded.

Service execution

Dispatch, route planning and rerouting, customer escalations, estimates, quality follow-through, technician readiness and coordinating the work when plans changed.

Business infrastructure

Systems and recordkeeping, operating procedures, vendors, purchasing, inventory, equipment and decisions balancing service quality, workload and expense.

My contribution was helping put the people, processes and operating rhythm in place—not simply keeping an existing schedule updated.

Multi-market team leadership

Safe Haven Security Services

I led a 15-person regional sales team across five markets, connecting recruiting, onboarding, coaching and field execution to the metrics the business cared about.

My responsibilities included staffing decisions, scheduling, performance management, training, KPI review, lead and installation coordination, customer/technician handoffs and follow-up when work stalled.

I used operational reporting and system records to make coaching and accountability more specific, including attention to installation quality rather than activity volume alone.

Leadership principle

Good operations require a clear owner, a measurable standard and a reliable process for exceptions. That's the same principle behind my current automation and implementation work.

How I measure a healthy operation

Service capacity, productivity, follow-through, first-contact resolution, repeat work, customer outcomes, response time, escalations, team quality and cost-to-serve. These are the management measures I use to diagnose opportunities; I do not present them as audited improvement percentages without underlying records.
