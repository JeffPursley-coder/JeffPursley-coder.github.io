Five targets met. Three stayed open.

At the simulated 90-day review, five of eight KPIs met target. Three did not: route changes were 7.9% against a 7.5% goal, closeout completeness was 95.4% against 96%, and weekly reporting preparation was 1.76 hours against 1.5. Rather than declaring the rollout a success and moving on, the case recommends reviewing route-change reasons, targeted closeout reinforcement, and remaining manual reporting steps.

Route stability, closeout completeness, and reporting effort still need attention. The recommended response is specific to those issues rather than a broad redesign of the process.

The 30/60/90 operating results are scenario results. The SQL, model design, KPI definitions, and analysis are the portfolio work.
