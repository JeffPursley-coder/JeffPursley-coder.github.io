About my work

My background is in operations. That shapes how I approach technology.

At Dragonfly Mosquito & Pest Specialists, I helped run a service business that grew to approximately 21 employees. At Safe Haven Security Services, I led a 15-person team across five markets. I have worked with staffing, scheduling, dispatch, customers, reporting, and the handoffs where work gets lost.

My approach to implementation starts with the operating problem: document requirements, decide what to automate, test the failure cases that matter, and prepare the people who will use the system. I also coordinated volunteer project work using Jira, Confluence and Scrum practices.

Education: Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (BSBA), Operations Management; Bachelor of Science (B.S.), Computer Information Systems — Thomas Edison State University. Certifications: PMP, CSM and CSPO.
