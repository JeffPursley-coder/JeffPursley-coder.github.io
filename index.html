About my work

My background is in operations. That shapes how I approach technology.

At Dragonfly Mosquito & Pest Specialists, I helped run a service business that grew to approximately 21 employees. At Safe Haven Security Services, I led a 15-person team across five markets. I have worked with staffing, scheduling, dispatch, customers, reporting, and the handoffs where work gets lost.

I now apply that experience to business systems and implementation. I want to understand the process first, document what needs to change, test the solution, and make sure people can actually use it. My recent projects include real Google Workspace automation, customer-workflow analysis, and an independent synthetic implementation and AI portfolio.

Education: Thomas Edison State University, B.S. in General Management and B.A. in Computer Science. Credentials: PMP, Certified ScrumMaster (CSM), Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO).
