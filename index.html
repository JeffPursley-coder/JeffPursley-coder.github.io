About Jeff

I’ve been responsible for running the business. I know what it takes to change how it runs.

At Dragonfly, I helped run the business side of a growing service operation, making decisions about hiring, compensation, pricing, payroll budgets, scheduling, equipment, customer delivery and operating costs while contributing to broader financial strategy.

At Safe Haven, I turned around an underperforming team, developed managers, shortened onboarding and created more repeatable management routines. Earlier at TogetherHealth, I ranked #1 individually among approximately 350 producers before supervising around 25 licensed professionals and leading a top-performing enrollment team.

Nonprofit project work, consulting and independent systems projects connect that experience to structured delivery. I hold degrees in Operations Management and Computer Information Systems, with PMP, CSM and CSPO certifications.

My professional focus is where operations leadership meets project execution and business systems improvement.
