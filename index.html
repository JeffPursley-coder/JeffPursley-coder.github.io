What makes my approach different

I have been responsible for the work—not just the software.

At Dragonfly Mosquito & Pest Specialists, I co-built and helped operate a service business that grew to approximately 21 employees. I worked across hiring, workforce planning, dispatch, routing, customer escalations, purchasing, payroll, performance management and daily operations. At Safe Haven, I led 15 people across five markets. At TogetherHealth, I supervised roughly 25 licensed professionals on a team that ranked first during the Annual Enrollment Period.

Those roles taught me what a broken handoff actually costs: missed commitments, repeat calls, wasted capacity and managers constantly chasing updates. I now use that knowledge to consult on workflows, organize implementation work and configure business systems that give teams a clearer picture of what happens next.

Business leadership + project discipline + practical AI. I combine approximately a decade of operational experience with two bachelor's degrees in operations management and computer information systems, PMP certification, and CSM/CSPO credentials.
