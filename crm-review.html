Customer Operations & CRM Workflow Review

A real business-workflow assessment spanning recorded conversations, call activity, frontline guidance and CRM handoffs. I developed recommendations to improve customer intake, escalation, accountability and management reporting.

Where the customer experience and the operating process were breaking down

Customer communications, advocacy review and commercial onboarding can be hard to coordinate when call context and business outcomes live in different systems. My review focused on how to distinguish new versus existing contacts, collect only necessary facts, screen urgency, route appropriately, and leave every interaction with a clear next owner.

My consulting contribution: I examined recorded-call patterns, communications analytics, training material and available CRM practices; mapped where important context could be lost; then designed structured intake questions, urgency screening, follow-up ownership guidance and a practical front-line call playbook.

The management decision behind my recommendation

A call system already captures conversations and communication activity. Re-entering those facts in another spreadsheet creates duplicate work. The CRM should capture business stage, the next owner, and the outcome that the call system cannot verify.

A clearer customer workflow from first call to accountable follow-through.

Process improvement

Working deliverables

A structured call-handling playbook covering intake, issue classification, urgency and risk-based handoff, onboarding questions, escalation boundaries, quality checks and follow-up expectations.

Data quality

Analytics interpretation

I separated activity metrics, such as calls, messages and contact attempts, from actual business results, including case stage, verified enrollment, resolution and next action. I recommended matching telephony evidence to CRM outcomes before drawing performance conclusions.

My key consulting recommendation

A call record confirms contact, not enrollment or resolution. I recommended keeping conversation history in the existing phone system while checking CRM stage, agreement status and the next owner before reporting conversion or case completion. This recommendation keeps contact history and verified business outcomes distinct.

The working call playbook also separates routine scheduling from sensitive advocacy decisions. Staff should capture the issue and deadline, then route high-impact questions to someone authorized to review them.

Consulting deliverables

I delivered a workflow assessment and practical recommendations for the responsible team, including intake guidance, escalation boundaries, follow-up ownership and clearer reporting logic. The organization is not named in this public case study.
